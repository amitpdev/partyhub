# Party Hub dashboard

Mode: Operate.

Audience: the host or DJ running music from a laptop in a dim room.
Job: understand and control now playing and the queue at a glance.
Primary action: search YouTube or add a direct link without leaving the playback context.
Constraints: laptop-hosted audio-only playback, optional guest contribution through ngrok, strong contrast, keyboard access, and explicit playback and helper states.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The queue is a live setlist, not a media-library dashboard. The screen refuses KPI tiles, promotional hero treatment, and decorative audio visualizers; now playing and queue order are the product.

OWN-WORLD: A neutral near-black ground, lightness-only elevation, white type, and one scarce theme accent. Midnight Violet is the default nightlife signal; Signal Orange, Electric Blue, Terminal Green, and High Contrast are curated DJ-local alternatives. Cover art supplies the remaining color. Rounded panels are broad working surfaces, never nested card decoration.

STORY: The DJ first sees exactly what is playing and whether playback is healthy. A separate Add your song card puts YouTube search first and direct-link entry directly above the ordered queue. Search opens a focused result dialog; errors explain recovery without taking over the screen.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact product header sits above a two-column work area. The content-sized left area is the now-playing deck with artwork, title, progress, and transport controls. The wider right area stacks a standalone Add your song card over an independently scrolling queue with five or more rows when data is available. The adder reads heading → Search YouTube → link alternative → guidance. A vertical setlist spine marks played, current, and queued positions without relying on color alone.

FORM: Code-led Operate surface. Taste style `true-black-flat`, seed 1124216377. Impeccable direction reference seed:8ec39d68cee65164. The signature move is the setlist spine: completion advances its bright current marker through a stable ordered line while frequent controls remain instant.
